HEALTH & NUTRITION HUB

Training is only 30% of the equation; proper recovery, metabolic fuel, micronutrient timing, and lifestyle habits construct the remaining 70%. Explore our science-backed guides.

80/20 Rule Principle

80% nutrient-dense single-ingredient foods, 20% flexibility to ensure lifelong consistency without burnout.

FOUNDATIONAL EATING

Fuel Clean. Perform Ruthlessly.

Your diet dictates hormonal output, muscle recovery velocity, and cognitive sharpness. Ditch the extreme crash diets and adopt sustainable, whole-food nourishment.

Prioritize High-Bioavailability Protein

Target 1.6g to 2.2g of protein per kg of bodyweight from eggs, lean poultry, wild fish, tofu, or whey to maintain positive nitrogen balance.

Color Your Plate With Phytonutrients

Include at least 3 distinct vegetable colors daily to supply antioxidants that eliminate exercise-induced systemic oxidative stress.

Eliminate Ultra-Processed Foods & Liquid Sugars

Minimize refined industrial seed oils, high-fructose corn syrups, and synthetic preservatives that trigger gut inflammation and fatigue.

The Macro Trinity

Protein builds tissue, Carbohydrates fuel glycolytic work, and Healthy Fats regulate vital hormones.

METABOLIC SCIENCE

Nutrient Timing & Macronutrient Splits

Understand the biochemical role of macronutrients and learn how to schedule meals around intense workout windows for optimum glycogen replenishment.

Pre-Workout Glycogen Top-Up (60-90 Min Prior)

Consume easily digestible complex carbs with a moderate protein portion (e.g., oatmeal with banana and whey) to sustain intra-workout ATP output.

Post-Workout Anabolic Window (Within 45 Min)

Combine rapid amino acids with fast-acting carbohydrates to spike insulin slightly and drive nutrients directly into depleted muscle fibers.

Essential Fatty Acids for Testosterone & Joints

Never drop dietary fats below 20% of total calories. Rely on avocados, extra virgin olive oil, and Omega-3 rich fish to protect hormonal health.

Consistency Beats Intensity

Small disciplined habits compounded daily yield monumental physical achievements over 12 months.

DAILY ROUTINES

Habits of High Performers

Your results are an echo of what you repeatedly practice every single morning and evening. Integrate these non-negotiable rituals.

Accumulate 8,000 to 10,000 NEAT Steps Daily

Non-Exercise Activity Thermogenesis (NEAT) burns more cumulative calories weekly than a 45-minute treadmill sprint. Walk after each meal!

Morning Mobility & Joint Flossing (10 Mins)

Perform cat-cows, deep Asian squats, and shoulder dislocates right after waking to lubricate synovial joints and correct posture.

Log Your Progressive Lifts & Nutrition

What gets measured gets improved. Track your weights, reps, and macros in the FITZONE mobile app to eliminate plateau guesswork.

2% Dehydration = 15% Power Loss

Hydrated muscle cells maintain higher cellular volume, fostering optimal protein synthesis and preventing cramps.

OPTIMAL HYDRATION

Water & Electrolyte Protocol

Water is the medium through which all cellular metabolic reactions occur. Sweating during heavy sessions drains crucial minerals: sodium, potassium, and magnesium.

Hydrate Immediately Upon Waking

Drink 500ml of room-temperature water with a pinch of Celtic sea salt to recharge adrenal glands and flush overnight cellular waste.

Intra-Workout Electrolyte Sipping

During workouts exceeding 50 minutes, sip water fortified with sodium and potassium to prevent central nervous system fatigue.

Quick Daily Water Goal: Rule of thumb: 35ml to 40ml per kg of body weight
3.5 – 4.5 Liters / Day

Growth Hormone Peak

Human Growth Hormone (HGH) surges primarily during Slow-Wave Deep Sleep. Without sleep, muscles do not repair.

SLEEP & LONGEVITY

Master Recovery: Sleep & Stress

You do not grow in the gym; you break down muscle in the gym. You grow while in deep, uninterrupted REM sleep. Prioritize your bedroom environment.

Strict 7.5 to 9 Hours of Sleep Opportunity

Maintain consistent sleep and wake times (even on weekends) to synchronize circadian clock genes and optimize natural testosterone.

Cool, Dark & Quiet Sleep Sanctuary

Keep your sleeping room between 18°C–20°C (65°F–68°F) with blackout blinds to trigger rapid deep sleep cycle transitions.

Digital Sunset 60 Minutes Before Bed

Avoid blue screens and harsh LEDs 1 hour before sleep to allow endogenous melatonin secretion to rise without interference.
